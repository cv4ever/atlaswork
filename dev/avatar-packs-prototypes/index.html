O · How you pick a pack

The real question is what a pack is: a shelf of extra faces you mix agent by agent, or a way of drawing the whole crew, like a theme.

O1 Shelves in All faces

  • For: no new setting; a pack can hold any cast (24 heroes need not match 24 manga faces); mix as you like.
  • Against: a mixed crew reads as a collage in stacks and chat rows; switching an agent's pack swaps their person too; every face in a pack is a new name to keep free or taken.

O2 One style for the crew Recommended

  • For: one tap redraws everyone, Atlas included, and each stays the same person (F2); look choices already live in Appearance; hover to try it on, as with themes.
  • Against: a pack ships only once it draws all 48 portraits and Atlas (the six silhouettes stay as they are), about nine sheets each; no way to make just Vital a hero.

O3 Crew style, and any agent can differ

  • For: O2's one-tap crew, plus an exception for fun (Vital as a hero, the rest in Storybook).
  • Against: one more control on the badge; the collage starts with the first exception; the same nine sheets per pack as O2.
